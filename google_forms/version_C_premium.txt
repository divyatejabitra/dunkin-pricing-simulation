--------------------------------------------------------------------

Q8 [Linear scale 1-5, required]

Q9 [Linear scale 1-5, required]

SECTION 4 - Pricing (THIS SECTION DIFFERS BY VERSION - this is Version C)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Q10 [Linear scale 1-5, required]

be to purchase it instead of your usual coffee?
1 = Very unlikely ... 5 = Very likely

Q11 [Linear scale 1-5, required]

be to purchase it instead of your usual latte?
1 = Very unlikely ... 5 = Very likely
